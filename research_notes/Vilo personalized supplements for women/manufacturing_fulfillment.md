Research conditions, so the report writer can weigh these notes: the work was done on 2026-11-20. Only WebSearch result summaries could be used. WebFetch failed for okcapsule.com and nutraingredients.com because those hosts are outside this session's allowed network domains, so no full pages were read. ## 1. Production models and who uses them

### Takeaway
In the US the only proven model at scale is model (a): stock capsules, tablets and softgels at fixed doses, assembled into personalized daily pouches. Care/of and Persona used it, and white-label vendors like OK Capsule and Persona's own white-label arm sell it. Truly compounded blends (Bioniq granules, Rootine microbeads) and 3D-printed gummies (Nourished) are made in proprietary plants in Europe or the UK. Rootine has reportedly paused its microbeads. Nourished raised money to expand into the US but had paused US orders over import rules. None of the compounded or printed models has a US contract option I could find. For Vilo, the realistic choices are (a) and (d), a hybrid of a base formula plus 2 to 5 add-on SKUs.

### Cited Findings
**(a) Stock SKUs assembled into daily packs**
- Care/of shipped daily portions "packaged in convenient sachets labeled with the user's name." — [Suggestic 2026 roundup](https://blog.suggestic.com/top-16-personalized-supplement-brands)
- Persona offers "custom daily-dose vitamin and supplement packs tailored to individual health needs and delivered via subscription." The company was founded as Vitamin Packs (Persona says 1999; other sources date the rebrand to 2016-2018, so the dates conflict). — [Persona white-label PR, Nov 2024](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/persona-nutrition-launches-white-labeling-service-for-brands-looking-to-expand-into-the-personalized-nutrition-subscription-market-302294913.html); [New Hope: Vitamin Packs rebrands as Persona](https://newhope.com/business-management/vitamin-packs-rebrands-as-persona-nutrition)
- Parata, a pharmacy automation vendor, markets its ATP pouch packagers to nutraceutical companies for "individualized packaging." The pitch covers visual inspection that "can make pouch verification up to 90% faster," variable pouch sizes that cut paper waste, and "highly customizable printing for pouch and card design." — [Parata nutraceutical solutions page](https://parata.com/who-we-help/solutions-for-the-nutraceutical-market/)
- A used-equipment dealer lists a "PARATA - Personalized Nutrition Packaging System," which suggests pharmacy pouch hardware has been resold into personalized nutrition. — [Star Industries listing](https://www.starind.com/listings/7783587-used-parata-personalized-nutrition-packaging-systems)

**(b) Truly compounded per-person blends**
- Bioniq: its granules are made in Switzerland, and "each unique formula is produced at our facility in the United Kingdom." The company cites one of 40 granule formulas built from up to 22 nutrients; other pages say "up to 35 possible ingredients," so the counts are inconsistent. It also claims "over 10 million combinations of nutrient granules." — [Bioniq site](https://www.bioniq.com/); [Bioniq stage site](https://bioniq-stage.webflow.io/)
- Bioniq is sold in 70+ countries, and its user base "grew more than five-fold in 2024" (company claim, no subscriber count given). In 2025 it launched "Build Your Own" formulas. — [Endurance Business, 2025](https://endurance.biz/2025/industry-news/bioniq-build-your-own-supplement-formulas/)
- Rootine (2019): a lab and manufacturing company in Austria, run by the co-founder separately from Rootine, made the microbeads. — [NutraIngredients 2019](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2019/01/30/Rootine-turns-pellets-into-hyper-personalized-multivitamins/). Rootine's FAQ instead says manufacturing happens at the co-founder's "licensed medical-grade genetics laboratory" and gives no location. — [Rootine FAQ](https://rootine.co/pages/faqs). The two accounts do not clearly match.
- A 2026 secondary source says Rootine "paused Smart Multivitamin microbeads Feb 2025; pivoting to drink mixes." I could not confirm this with a primary source. — [Suggestic 2026](https://blog.suggestic.com/top-16-personalized-supplement-brands)

**(c) 3D-printed gummies (Nourished / Rem3dy Health, UK)**
- Rem3dy Health raised £14M (about $18.7M) at an £84M valuation to expand into the US, MENA and India. It produces about 500,000 personalized gummies per day in-house in Birmingham, UK, and holds 29 patents. — [BeautyMatter](https://beautymatter.com/articles/rem3dy-health-lands-14-million-to-scale-3d-printed-personalized-supplements); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/healthcare/articles/personalised-gummies-firm-rem3dy-health-140557970.html)
- A note on Nourished's US website said "import regulations brought in last year" forced it to pause US orders in August. I could not confirm whether US orders have resumed. — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/healthcare/articles/personalised-gummies-firm-rem3dy-health-140557970.html)
- A secondary source reports revenue of more than £10M in 2025 (611% year-on-year growth), with the new funds going to automation and advanced manufacturing. — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/healthcare/articles/personalised-gummies-firm-rem3dy-health-140557970.html); background on the process: [Healthcare Packaging](https://www.healthcarepackaging.com/machinery/robotics-automation/article/22301591/nourisheds-3dprinted-supplement-stacks-deliver-personalized-nutrients), [4Additive](https://www.4additive.com/en/nourished-3d-printed-custom-supplements-scaling/)

**(d) Hybrid: base formula plus add-ons**
- OK Capsule offers both "Curated Packs," priced from a catalog, and "Personalized Packs," estimated with a pack calculator. A brand can therefore run a fixed base pack with personalized add-ons through one vendor. — [OK Capsule pricing](https://okcapsule.squarespace.com/pricing)

### Inferences
- **Equipment and capital by model:**
  - (a) needs a pouch packager with one canister per SKU, plus a printer and a vision inspection system. Used Parata units have sold for $2.5k-$50k (see section 3). New units are quote-only.
  - (b) needs powder or granule dosing, weighing and blending per person, plus stability data for every possible combination. Bioniq gets around this by pre-making a fixed set of granule formulas (about 40) and combining them. That is effectively model (a) using granules instead of capsules.
  - (c) needs proprietary printers and patents (Nourished holds 29). The gummy format also has well-known moisture and shelf-life limits.
- **Accuracy and dose verification:** model (a) is easiest to verify. Each component is a released, tested finished unit, and the per-pack check is a count-and-identity vision inspection, which Parata markets. Models (b) and (c) mix ingredients per person, so each personal formula is effectively its own batch. Under Part 111 that makes per-pack specifications, testing and expiry dating much harder. This is my reasoning from the cGMP structure in section 5, not a cited FDA position.
- **Recommendation:** Vilo should start with (a) or (d). Model (b) is achievable later only as Bioniq does it, combining a fixed set of pre-made, pre-tested granule formulas. Model (c) is not practical in the US today: no US contract source exists and the UK leader paused US orders.

### Gaps
- No published per-unit cost, accuracy tolerance or shelf-life data for Bioniq granules, Rootine microbeads or Nourished gummies.
- I found no US contract manufacturer offering true per-person compounding, such as per-customer powder blends or liquid concentrates.
- The exact import rule behind Nourished's US pause was not confirmed. It may be the end of the US de minimis exemption in August 2025, but that is my guess and was not verified.
- Rootine's 2025 pause is single-sourced.

## 2. Contract manufacturers and personalized-pack fulfillment providers (US)

### Takeaway
A small set of vendors produce per-customer packs on demand. OK Capsule (API and Shopify, no minimums, 24-hour turnaround) and Persona's white-label service (launched Nov 2024) are turnkey. Vitalabs and Designs for Health offer low-minimum packet programs, but those are fixed combinations, not per-customer packs. Ordinary capsule contract manufacturers make the stock SKUs, with MOQs of roughly 1,000-5,000 bottles per SKU or 100k-350k bulk capsules. I found no public record of who manufactured for Care/of or Persona.

### Cited Findings
**Turnkey per-customer pack vendors**
- **OK Capsule:**
  - Produces "personalized supplement packets on demand with a 24-hour turnaround with low minimums." It raised $9.5M in 2022. — [NutraIngredients-USA 2022](https://nutraingredients-usa.com/Article/2022/03/24/personalized-vitamin-company-scores-9.5m-investment); [OK Capsule pricing](https://okcapsule.squarespace.com/pricing)
  - Pricing is "a simple per-order fee… no minimums, no inventory risk."
  - Its API onboarding page lists a packaging fee "starting at $21 per unit," supplement fees that vary by order contents, and shipping "starting at $7.50 per unit" not included in catalog prices. "Packaging fees decrease as your volume grows." — [OK Capsule API instructions](https://okcapsule.squarespace.com/api-instructions)
  - It integrates via API, MCP or Shopify. The Shopify app is listed at $100/month on one page; another listing says it is free to install. — [Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/ok-capsule-pack-builder)
  - Example B2B partner: JumpstartMD (2022). — [NutraIngredients 2022](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2022/09/07/JumpstartMD-OK-Capsule-join-forces-to-make-personalization-accessible)
- **Persona white-label:**

No public pricing or MOQ. — [PR Newswire](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/persona-nutrition-launches-white-labeling-service-for-brands-looking-to-expand-into-the-personalized-nutrition-subscription-market-302294913.html); [Persona white-label page](https://www.personanutrition.com/whitelabel)

- **Superior Supplement Mfg:** 2,500 units of 60-count bottles. For bulk capsules, one page says 150,000 and the FAQ says 350,000 (conflicting). — [Superior](https://www.superiorsupplementmfg.com/custom-capsule-manufacturing/)

- **Matsun Nutrition (liquids, gummies, powders):** 2,500 bottles minimum. — [Matsun](https://matsunnutrition.com/private-label-vitamins-low-minimum/)
- **SMP Nutra** advertises "low MOQs" and 800+ ready formulas but publishes no figure. **Makers Nutrition** advertises low MOQs on a page about 5 years old. — [SMP Nutra](https://smpnutra.com/); [Makers Nutrition](https://www.makersnutrition.com/vitamin-manufacturer)

### Inferences
- OK Capsule's published floor of $21 packaging + $7.50 shipping (about $28.50 per pack before contents) is a useful MVP benchmark. Outsourced per-customer pack assembly costs roughly 5-10x what in-house pouching costs at scale (see section 4). The trade-off is no capex and no inventory.
- Packet programs like Vitalabs, Designs for Health and DaVinci produce identical packets in volume, not per-customer packs. They could supply the base pack in the hybrid model (d), but not the personalization.

### Gaps
- No published MOQs, lead times or pricing were found for Vitaquest, Lief Labs, NutraScience Labs, Robinson Pharma, Bactolac or Gemini Pharmaceuticals; my searches returned nothing on them. Quotes would be needed.
- I could not find who manufactured or packed for Care/of, Persona or Rootine (US). The only exception is Rootine's 2019 Austrian lab. Nothing public turned up on in-house versus contract production for Care/of or Persona.
- I found no published lead times for per-customer pack vendors other than OK Capsule's "24-hour turnaround."

## 3. Automated pill-pack / multi-dose packaging equipment

### Takeaway
Pharmacy strip and pouch packagers (Parata/BD, Yuyama, Tosho, JVM, TCGRx) can pack supplements, and Parata explicitly markets to nutraceutical brands. New-machine prices are quote-only. Used units sell cheaply ($2.5k-$50k), and consumables (pouch paper and film) are available from third parties. Published throughput data is thin: the only figure found was 10-20 pouches per minute for a small generic unit.

### Cited Findings
- Parata's nutraceutical page offers "affordable packagers in varying sizes" sized to volume and growth plans, with vision verification and custom pouch printing. — [Parata](https://parata.com/who-we-help/solutions-for-the-nutraceutical-market/)
- BD Parata ATP 2 configurations: 64, 128, 192, 256, 320, 400 or 480 canisters, installable "in most facilities without needing construction." Its dual tray system allows continuous running during swaps. The PASS 36 is a small, low-cost entry unit. — [BD ATP 2 page](https://www.bd.com/en-ca/products-and-solutions/products/product-page.901-0238); [Cardinal Health Parata literature](https://www.cardinalhealth.com/content/dam/corp/web/documents/literature/cardinal-health-parata-systems.pdf)
- Used prices (2025-2026 listings or closes, asking prices not new-machine prices):
  - BD Parata ATP2 closed at $2,500 at a US government surplus auction. — [GSA/PPMS auction](https://ppms.gov/auctions/preview/341998)
  - Parata PASS 500 at $6,000, Parata PASS 208 at $10,000, and a JVM/Parata automatic tablet dispensing and packaging system at $49,900. — [eBay listings](https://www.ebay.com/shop/parata-machine?_nkw=parata+machine)
- A generic JVM/TCG-style small pouch packer (AG-40P) is rated at "10-20 bags/min." This is a marketplace listing, not a manufacturer specification. — [Alibaba](https://m.alibaba.com/product/1600484075402/JVM-TCG-Parata-High-Quality-Automatic.html)
- Consumables for Parata, TCGRx, Talyst and JVM packagers are sold by third parties such as PAS Packaging. Paper is also sold for Yuyama, Tosho (Topra, Xana-2400), JVM and Baxter FDS 300 machines. — [PAS Packaging](https://www.paspackaging.com/categories/products/strip-pouch-packaging-supplies.html); [Made-in-China listing](https://www.made-in-china.com/showroom/bigrain11/product-detailGqmJkdiCgjTt/China-Packing-Paper-for-Automated-Medication-Dispensing-Machine-Tosho-.html)
- The dispensing pharmacy packaging machine market is about $2.58B (2026), with Yuyama, Tosho and JVM named as key players. — [The Business Research Company](https://www.thebusinessresearchcompany.com/report/dispensing-pharmacy-packaging-machine-global-market-report)

### Inferences
- **Throughput sizing (using the conservative 10-20 pouches/min figure):**
  - 10,000 customers × 30 pouches = 300,000 pouches per month.
  - At 15/min that is about 333 machine-hours a month, or about 2 machines on one 8-hour shift over 21 working days.
  - At 1,000 customers (30,000 pouches) one entry-level machine runs only about 33 hours a month. Owning equipment below roughly 2,000-3,000 subscribers is hard to justify against outsourcing.
- **SKU breadth:** canister count caps it. A 64-canister ATP is plenty for a 25-40 SKU formulary. Each SKU usually needs a canister calibrated to that tablet or capsule size, and calibrating canisters is a hidden setup cost.
- **Softgels and probiotics:** large fish-oil softgels and heat- or moisture-sensitive probiotics are hard for many canister systems. This is an industry generality I did not verify. A common workaround is a separate blister or bottle for omega-3 and probiotics.
- **Who uses these machines:** I found no named supplement brand that does. Persona's and Care/of's equipment is not publicly documented.

### Gaps
- No new-machine list prices for Parata, Yuyama, Tosho, JVM or Swisslog. No Omnicell/Swisslog nutraceutical material was found.
- No manufacturer-published throughput for ATP or Tosho units. Pharmacy-grade units are commonly said to run much faster than 10-20 per minute, but I did not verify that.
- No per-pouch consumable cost for paper or film was found.

## 4. Cost of goods benchmarks

### Takeaway
No personalized-supplement company discloses per-pack COGS. My estimates, built from cited inputs:
- **Outsourced to a pack vendor (MVP):** about $35-45 per customer-month landed. That is $21+ pack fee, about $6-15 contents and $7.50+ shipping.
- **In-house or dedicated contract pouching at scale:** about $17-35 per customer-month landed. Postage of about $6-9 and fulfillment of about $2-5 are the largest fixed pieces.

Raw nutrient material is cheap. Unit count and pack labor drive the cost.

### Cited Findings
- OK Capsule: packaging fee from $21 per unit, supplement fees by contents, shipping from $7.50 per unit. — [OK Capsule API page](https://okcapsule.squarespace.com/api-instructions)
- Raw material retail-bulk prices (undated pages, likely 2025-2026; not industrial contract prices):
  - Vitamin D3 powder: $60.97/kg at BulkSupplements (a diluted, "50 mg per serving" product) and $1,224.95 per 25 kg at PureBulk, about $49-61/kg. — [PureBulk D3](https://purebulk.com/products/cholecalciferol-vitamin-d3-bulk)
  - Magnesium glycinate: $34.97/kg or $379.29 per 25 kg (about $15/kg) at BulkSupplements, a $224.75 per 25 kg wholesale listing (about $9/kg), and PureBulk bisglycinate at $883.75 per 25 kg (about $35/kg). — [BulkSupplements](https://www.bulksupplements.com/products/magnesium-glycinate-powder); [BulkSupplements wholesale](https://wholesale.bulksupplements.com/products/magnesium-glycinate-powder); [PureBulk](https://purebulk.com/products/magnesium-glycinate-bulk)
  - Fish oil softgels, 1,000 mg triple strength: $409.50 per 6,000 at Jedwards International, about $0.068 per softgel. — [Bulk Natural Oils/Jedwards](https://bulknaturaloils.com/fish-oil-capsules-triple-strength-1000mg-fg5127.html)
- Consumer price context, not COGS:
  - Persona's average customer is estimated to pay about $69/month. — [HealthRx pricing analysis](https://healthrx.com/brands-persona/pricing-analysis)
  - Care/of was about $35-70/month (single unsourced blog table). — [MySubscriptionAddiction](https://www.mysubscriptionaddiction.com/best-subscription-boxes/best-vitamin-supplement-subscriptions)
- Margin claims, all weak:
  - A market report claims "35-50% margins" for digital-first players like Care/of and Persona, with no stated methodology. — [Dataintelo](https://dataintelo.com/report/personalized-supplement-subscription-market)
  - A business-plan blog cites 45-65% gross margin targets for DTC vitamin subscriptions. — [Financialmodel.net](https://financialmodel.net/blogs/owner-makes/personalized-supplements-shop)

### Inferences
These are estimates from my own arithmetic, not quotes.
- **Raw nutrient cost is trivial for most actives:**
  - D3 2,000 IU: assuming the retail powder is a 100,000 IU/g dilution, which is consistent with "50 mg per serving," a dose is about 20 mg, or about $0.001/day ($0.04/month).
  - Magnesium: 200 mg elemental Mg from bisglycinate (about 14% Mg, a chemistry assumption) is about 1.4 g/day. At $9-35/kg that is about $0.40-1.50/month. The real cost is that it takes 2-3 capsules a day, which raises unit count and pack volume.
  - Omega-3: 1-2 softgels/day at about $0.07 is about $2-4/month.
- **Contents of a 4-8 unit/day pack (120-240 units/month):** at an assumed $0.03-0.08 per finished unit, about $4-19/month, central estimate $6-15. Probiotics, and branded forms such as methylfolate and iron bisglycinate, push toward the top of the range. No price data was found for those.
- **Packaging per month:** 30 printed pouches, box or dispenser, insert and mailer. Estimated $1.50-3.00 in-house; no source found.
- **Landed COGS per customer-month:**
  - Outsourced MVP (OK Capsule-type): $21 + $6-15 + $7.50 = about **$35-44**, before platform fees and returns or reships.
  - In-house at 10k+ customers: contents $6-15 + packaging $1.50-3 + pouching labor and depreciation $1-3 + pick/pack $2-5 + postage $6-9 = about **$17-35**.
- **Margin check:** at Persona-like $60-70/month prices, outsourced COGS of about $40 leaves only about 35-40% gross margin before customer acquisition cost (CAC). That is probably not sustainable, so Vilo has to move production in-house or into a dedicated contract arrangement by roughly the 2,000-5,000 subscriber level.

### Gaps
- No bulk price data was found for methylfolate (5-MTHF salts), iron bisglycinate or probiotics, and no CMO price per 1,000 finished capsules. Quotes are needed.
- No published pouch, box or insert cost per unit.
- No disclosed COGS from Care/of, Persona, Rootine or Bioniq.

## 5. Quality and compliance

### Takeaway
Vilo, as the "own-label distributor" whose name is on the label, is "ultimately responsible" under 21 CFR 111 even if everything is outsourced. It needs:
- its own quality unit
- contractor qualification (audit plus quality agreement)
- review of master manufacturing records
- a serious adverse event (SAE) reporting system (15 business days to FDA, via MedWatch 3500A)
- heavy-metals control for Prop 65 (lead safe harbor 0.5 µg/day)

Third-party certification takes months and costs five figures. Budget roughly $15k-80k in year 1 for NSF Certified for Sport across a small line. NSF/ANSI 455-2 takes 4-18 months.

### Cited Findings
**Part 111 duties of the own-label distributor**
- FDA warning letters describe the own-label distributor as "ultimately responsible" for product quality and for ensuring all production phases comply with Part 111. — [SupplySide SJ](https://www.supplysidesj.com/manufacturing/own-label-distributors-and-contract-manufacturers-a-complex-relationship-requiring-mutual-support)
- A 2013 FDA warning letter said the distributor must know how its product is manufactured in order to decide whether it is adulterated. — [NutraIngredients 2013](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2013/05/10/FDA-warning-letter-highlights-obligations-of-private-label-distributors/)
- The distributor needs a process to qualify contractors, typically an initial audit plus ongoing monitoring, and a quality agreement. — [SupplySide SJ, OLD quality systems](https://www.supplysidesj.com/manufacturing/essential-quality-systems-for-an-own-label-distributor); [SupplySide SJ, contract manufacturer requirements](https://www.supplysidesj.com/manufacturing/gmp-and-other-requirements-for-dietary-supplement-contract-manufacturers)
- A warning letter cited a brand holder for not knowing the master manufacturing record and for having no batch production record for a lot. — [Trust Transparency](https://trusttransparency.com/a-must-read-fda-warning-letter-that-truly-inspires/)
- Brands must maintain their own quality unit (Subpart F), and FDA is scrutinizing it. If the contract manufacturer labels the product, both parties share responsibility for label content. — [SupplySide SJ cGMPs with TLC](https://www.supplysidesj.com/labeling/cgmps-with-tlc-own-label-distributor-responsibilities); [Certified Laboratories](https://certified-laboratories.com/blog/own-label-distribution/)

**Adverse event reporting (Dietary Supplement and Nonprescription Drug Consumer Protection Act, 21 U.S.C. 379aa-1)**
- The "responsible person" must submit serious adverse event reports to FDA within 15 business days of receipt, and follow-up information within 15 business days, for up to 1 year. The duty falls on the manufacturer, packer or distributor named on the label. Reports use MedWatch Form 3500A. — [21 U.S.C. 379aa-1 (Cornell LII)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/21/379aa-1); [SupplySide SJ](https://www.supplysidesj.com/regulatory/adverse-event-reporting-requirements-dietary-supplements)

**Third-party certification (cost and timeline)**
- NSF/ANSI 455-2:
  - Cost and duration are "similar to NSF/ANSI 173." Listing can happen "in as little as 4 months," and the process can take up to 18 months. — [NSF 455 FAQ](https://d2evkimvhatqav.cloudfront.net/documents/hs_dce_1911_gmp_455_faq_s.pdf)
  - A consultant estimate (BSCG) is 3-6 months with existing quality systems and 6-9 months from scratch. — [BSCG](https://www.bscg.org/blogs/single/gmp-certification-cost-for-supplement-brands-fees-factors-and-budget-ranges)
  - Annual surveillance audits and a roughly 3-year recertification cycle (vendor source). — [V5 Ultimate](https://v5ultimate.com/guides/nsf-ansi-455-2-supplement-gmp-readiness)
  - NSF publishes no fee schedule.
- NSF Certified for Sport, from consultant and comparison-site estimates that conflict by 2x or more (I could not verify which page gave which figure): about $1,500-4,000 initial per product and $15k-30k+ for a line, or $40k-80k in year 1 for a 4-SKU brand, or $5k-10k per product initial plus $600-1,200 per lot for banned-substance testing. — [Triton Nutra](https://tritonnutragroup.com/blog/nsf-certification-cost); [V5 Ultimate comparison](https://v5ultimate.com/guides/supplement-third-party-testing-compared); [Nutrola 2026](https://nutrola.app/en/blog/usp-nsf-informed-sport-consumerlab-certifications-compared-2026)
- USP Verified, same caveats: plant audit $20k-40k initial plus $10k-20k annual; per-product verification $5k-10k plus lot testing. Another site says $30k-100k+ per product per year. — [V5 Ultimate comparison](https://v5ultimate.com/guides/supplement-third-party-testing-compared); [Nutrola 2026](https://nutrola.app/en/blog/usp-nsf-informed-sport-consumerlab-certifications-compared-2026)

**Prop 65 / heavy metals**
- The Prop 65 safe harbor for lead in supplements is 0.5 µg/day, calculated from concentration × serving size × daily servings. — [Eurofins](https://www.eurofins.com/en-us/food-feed-testing/resource-library/proposition-65-in-food-dietary-supplements-key-risks-limits-and-compliance/); [CA AG consent judgment](https://oag.ca.gov/system/files/prop65/judgments/2021-01974J4853.pdf)
- Minimum test panel: lead, total and speciated arsenic, cadmium and mercury. — [Qalitex](https://qalitex-website.pages.dev/blog/prop-65-heavy-metal-limits-dietary-supplements-amazon-sellers/)
- Heavy-metals testing "averages a mere $100 per sample" (older trade article). — [New Hope](https://www.newhope.com/food-and-beverage/toxins-public-health-scourge-and-financial-disaster)

### Inferences
- **Prop 65 for personalized packs:** exposure is per daily pack, not per SKU. A woman taking 6-8 units a day sums the lead from every component. Vilo's formulation engine should hold a per-SKU lead (µg per unit) value from each certificate of analysis and cap the daily total below 0.5 µg, or label accordingly.
- **Traceability per customer:** each pouch or box record should link customer ID, pack date, and the lot number and expiry of every component SKU, plus the packager and canister ID. Without this, an SAE or recall can't be traced. Parata-type systems print per-pouch data, which supports this.
- **Expiry dating:** the pack's expiry should be the earliest expiry among its components. If stock SKUs carry stability data in their original containers, Vilo needs a stability rationale, or a stability study, for the pouch film, especially for hygroscopic items and probiotics.
- **Certification sequencing:** NSF/ANSI 455-2 applies to manufacturing sites. A brand normally requires its CMO and packager to hold it rather than certifying itself. NSF Certified for Sport or USP Verified for a variable per-person product is impractical. Certifying the 20-40 component SKUs is the realistic path.

### Gaps
I could not run searches to verify the items below, so they are unsourced background for the writer to confirm:
- **Identity testing:** 21 CFR 111.75(a)(1) requires at least one identity test on each dietary ingredient lot before use. Finished-batch specifications fall under 111.70 and 111.75(c).
- **Stability:** FDA does not mandate expiry dating, but any expiry date stated must be supported by data.
- **Returns:** Subpart N covers returned supplements, which must be quarantined and either destroyed or salvaged after quality-unit review.
- **Facility registration:** FDA food facility registration (biennial renewal Oct-Dec of even years, so 2026 is a renewal year) applies to facilities that manufacture, pack or hold. A brand that only owns labels may not need to register, but its packer and 3PL do.
- **Label address:** the domestic address or phone number for AE reports must appear on the label, and SAE records must be kept 6 years.
- **New Dietary Ingredient (NDI) notification** may be needed for novel ingredients.
- **Prop 65 testing costs:** current lab price lists were not found; the $100 figure is old.

## 6. Fulfillment and shipping

### Takeaway
A 30-day pouch box weighs roughly 6-12 oz. It ships USPS Ground Advantage commercial for about $6-9 depending on zone and on which 2026 price list is used. 3PL pick/pack runs about $2-5 per order, with $0.75-3.00 extra for kitting. Expect monthly minimums of $500-2,500 and setup fees of $250-1,000. Softgels soften above about 85°F and probiotics are the most heat-sensitive items. In summer, plan insulated liners and avoid shipping late in the week, or isolate those SKUs.

### Cited Findings
**USPS Ground Advantage (8 oz, commercial)**
- Oct 4, 2026 price list as reproduced by third-party sites from USPS Notice 123: $7.33 (zone 1) to $8.95 (zone 8). Retail 8 oz is $8.40-$10.20. — [USPS Notice 123](https://pe.usps.com/text/dmm300/notice123.htm); [DimMath](https://dimmath.com/blog/usps-ground-advantage-rates-zone-chart-weight-breaks/)
- Conflicting earlier 2026 figures:
  - $6.93-$8.40 for anything 4 oz-15.999 oz (the "July 12, 2026" list). — [Flipper Tools](https://flippertools.com/tools/usps-ground-advantage-calculator)
  - $6.03-$6.74 (April 26, 2026 list). — [ResaleMargin](https://www.resalemargin.com/usps-ground-advantage-rates)
  - About $6.24 for 8 oz to zone 8 via Shippo (May 2026). — [Shippo](https://goshippo.com/blog/usps-parcel-select-ground-cost-sizes-and-how-it-works)
- Commercial prices for 4, 8, 12 and 15.999 oz are now identical within each zone. — [Flipper Tools](https://flippertools.com/tools/usps-ground-advantage-calculator)

**3PL costs (2026 survey-based guides; secondary sources that disagree slightly)**
- Pick/pack: about $2-5 per order; survey averages $3.20-3.21 for a one-item B2C order; extra items about $0.48-1.50 each. — [Fulfill.com](https://www.fulfill.com/3pl-pricing); [WarehousingCosts](https://warehousingcosts.com/3pl/costs); [Catalist](https://catalistgroup.co/blog/3pl-fulfillment-cost-breakdown-2026/)
- Kitting or subscription assembly adds $0.75-3.00 per order. Complex kits of 4+ items cost $5-12 per kit. — [Rush Order](https://rushorder.com/blog/how-much-does-a-3pl-cost); [Morsa](https://morsa.ai/guides/3pl-pricing)
- Monthly minimums average $752 (range $500-2,500). Q4 peak surcharges run 15-30%. About half of 3PLs charge setup fees of $250-1,000. — [Fulfill.com](https://www.fulfill.com/3pl-pricing); [Catalist](https://catalistgroup.co/blog/3pl-fulfillment-cost-breakdown-2026/)
- Supplement pick/pack runs above general ecommerce because of lot-level pick verification. Climate-controlled storage is needed for probiotics, gummies, softgels and liquids. — [eFulfillment Service, Sept 2026](https://www.efulfillmentservice.com/2026/09/supplement-fulfillment-cost-guide/)

**Heat sensitivity**
- Softgels can soften and stick together in heat. Fullscript typically ships softgels without ice packs, per supplier guidance. — [Fullscript](https://support.fullscript.com/articles/heat-sensitive-products)
- Heat damage is most common in summer ground shipping above 85°F. — [BodyBio](https://bodybio.com/pages/shipping-in-summer-months-faq)
- Heat can break down softgel shells and speed oxidation. — [ConsumerLab](https://www.consumerlab.com/answers/do-supplements-lose-effectiveness-in-the-heat/vitamin_temperature)
- USPS shipping in summer, or to the southern US, risks temperature spikes that can inactivate probiotics. — [ExeGi white paper](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0736/3645/files/IMP-07878_-_ExeGi_Pharma_Cold_Chain_White_Paper_Web_db2661b7-7a78-4f23-80c4-f7a7ffbce01c.pdf)
- Pure Encapsulations never ships probiotics on Thursday or Friday, and found foam refrigerant bricks beat standard ice packs. — [Pure Encapsulations Pro](https://blog.pureencapsulationspro.com/?p=404)
- Gel packs last about 24-48 hours, so use overnight or 2-day shipping in heat. — [Speed Commerce](https://www.speedcommerce.com/insights/how-to-ship-vitamins-nutraceuticals-pharmaceuticals-supplements-a-complete-guide/)
- Moisture is more damaging to probiotics than short heat exposure, and long-term storage matters most. — [Fullscript](https://support.fullscript.com/articles/heat-sensitive-products)

### Inferences
- **Package weight:** 180 units × about 0.6-1.0 g, plus pouches, box, insert and mailer, comes to about 200-350 g (7-12 oz). Every 4-15.999 oz package costs the same commercial rate, so packaging weight under 1 lb barely affects postage. Zone mix does. A central-US fulfillment site keeps the average zone and transit time down.
- **Budget per shipped box:** about $7.50-8.50 postage under the Oct 2026 list, plus $3-6 for 3PL pick/pack and kitting.
- **Probiotics, best option:** choose shelf-stable, spore-based or freeze-dried strains in moisture-protective blisters or desiccated packs, so no cold chain is needed. Otherwise use foam bricks, insulated mailers and no Thursday/Friday shipping from about May to September. Cold packs could add several dollars per box; no source found for that figure.
- **Returns:** personalized packs can't be resold. Policy should be refund or reship plus documented destruction, logged for any linked SAE review.

### Gaps
- UPS Ground Saver (which replaced SurePost) and FedEx Ground Economy 2026 rates for 4-8 oz were not retrieved.
- No research done on international shipping limits (country import rules for supplements, de minimis changes). That needs a follow-up.
- No named 3PLs with personalized-pack subscription experience were confirmed beyond Priority Fulfillment and eFulfillment Service. ShipBob, ShipMonk and Red Stag were not verified.
- No cost data for insulated mailers or cold packs.

## 7. Inventory and working capital

### Takeaway
In the pack model, inventory is a formulary of roughly 20-40 stock SKUs held as bulk finished units. CMO MOQs of 100k-350k capsules (or 1,000-5,000 bottles) per custom SKU tie up cash and create expiry risk for slow-moving SKUs. MVP vendors like OK Capsule take on the inventory risk ("no minimums, no inventory risk") in exchange for higher per-pack fees.

### Cited Findings
- Bulk capsule MOQs per custom SKU: 150k-350k (Superior), under 100k (Eagle Works), and 5,000 units per SKU custom or 1,000 stock (Sun Nutra). — [Superior](https://www.superiorsupplementmfg.com/custom-capsule-manufacturing/); [Eagle Works](https://eagleworksmfg.com/low-moq/); [Sun Nutra](https://sunnutra.com/low-moq-supplement-manufacturer)
- OK Capsule offers "no minimums, no inventory risk." — [OK Capsule pricing](https://okcapsule.squarespace.com/pricing)
- A 64-480 canister ATP 2 configuration sets the maximum SKU count on one machine. — [BD ATP 2](https://www.bd.com/en-ca/products-and-solutions/products/product-page.901-0238)

### Inferences
- **Formulary math:** 30 SKUs × about 150k units × about $0.03-0.05 per unit is about $135k-225k of inventory at CMO minimums. Long-tail SKUs used by under 5% of customers could take years to deplete. Most capsule products carry roughly 2-3 year shelf life; probiotics are often shorter. The risk is write-offs.
- **Mitigations:**
  - Start with a 15-25 SKU formulary.
  - Buy long-tail SKUs as stock white-label items (MOQ about 1,000 bottles) instead of custom runs.
  - Recompute dosing so most personalization uses multiple units of the same SKU, for example 1-3 × D3 1,000 IU, instead of many dose strengths.
  - Forecast SKU demand from the algorithm's output distribution, which is predictable because the personalization is deterministic.
  - Set reorder points on component expiry, not only quantity.
- **Working capital with the hybrid model (d):** most volume is in the standardized base (one high-volume SKU), so MOQs are absorbed quickly. Only 2-5 add-ons per customer come from the long-tail formulary.

### Gaps
- No data on ingredient expiry or waste rates, or on forecasting practice, at Care/of, Persona or Bioniq.

## 8. What went wrong for failed players, and what scaled players do

### Takeaway
Care/of, the best-known US pack brand, was shut down by Bayer in June 2024 (143 layoffs, all subscriptions cancelled). Coverage blames rising competition and Bayer's capital reallocation. I found no detailed operational post-mortem. The assignment's premise that Baze and Gainful failed is not supported: Nature's Way acquired Baze, and Gainful reports profitability and Target distribution. Persona survives: it went from Nestlé ownership back to its founders (single source) and expanded into white-label and practitioner channels. Bioniq and Nourished scale with proprietary European and UK plants. Rootine reportedly paused microbeads.

### Cited Findings
- **Care/of:**

- **Gainful:** profitable since early 2023, Series B in Sept 2024, sold in 1,800+ Target stores (company and secondary sources). — [YesPress](https://yespress.io/gainful)

### Inferences
- **Business-model pattern:** both big pack brands pushed toward standardized retail SKUs and B2B channels. Care/of went to Target; Persona went to white-label, practitioners and a new women's brand. That pattern points to per-customer pack economics being hard on their own at consumer price points. My guess is that this pressure comes from high pack-assembly cost plus customer acquisition cost (CAC) and churn, not from the manufacturing technology itself.
- **The Care/of lesson for Vilo:** the parent's capital decision was fatal. A personalized pack operation has high fixed costs (a quality unit, packaging lines, a formulary), so it needs either scale or a partner whose fixed costs are shared, which is the white-label model.

## 9. Recommended phased path (MVP vs scale) with estimated costs

### Takeaway
- **Phase 1 (100-1,000 customers):** use a white-label per-customer pack vendor (OK Capsule API or Persona white-label) with zero inventory. Landed COGS is about $35-45 per customer-month. Upfront spend is mostly quality and regulatory setup (estimate about $30k-80k).
- **Phase 2 (1,000-10,000):** move to hybrid (d). Commission one or two standardized base SKUs from a CMO (MOQ about 2,500-5,000 units) and keep pack assembly outsourced or move it to a dedicated contract packer.
- **Phase 3 (10,000+):** bring pouching in-house or into a dedicated cGMP packer with Parata-class equipment. Landed COGS is about $17-35. Plan for a 20-40 SKU formulary, about $150k-250k of inventory, and NSF/ANSI 455-2 at the packing site.

### Cited Findings
- OK Capsule: no minimums, 24-hour turnaround, $21+ pack fee, $7.50+ shipping. — [OK Capsule pricing](https://okcapsule.squarespace.com/pricing); [API page](https://okcapsule.squarespace.com/api-instructions)
- Persona white-label is "turnkey" and "flexible format for partners with varying budgets." — [PR Newswire](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/persona-nutrition-launches-white-labeling-service-for-brands-looking-to-expand-into-the-personalized-nutrition-subscription-market-302294913.html)
- CMO MOQs, pouch-packager used prices, certification timelines and 3PL and postage costs: see sections 2-6 for sources.

### Inferences
All dollar figures here are estimates built on the cited inputs. None are quotes.

**Phase 1 MVP (0-1,000 subscribers, months 0-6)**
- **Production:** OK Capsule API or Persona white-label, with Vilo's formulation engine sending per-customer pack specs. Vet the vendor's formulary so its SKUs cover Vilo's nutrient and dose map.
- **Capex:** about $0.
- **One-time setup, about $30k-80k:**
  - regulatory and label review
  - quality agreement plus a supplier audit, by a consultant or in person
  - SOPs for the Vilo quality unit, complaint handling and SAE (MedWatch) reporting
  - product liability insurance
  - Prop 65 assessment of vendor certificates of analysis
- **Ongoing:** about $35-45 per customer-month landed. At 1,000 subscribers that is about $35k-45k a month in COGS.
- **Lead time:** integration plus formulary mapping takes weeks, not months. Vendor turnaround is about 24 hours (OK Capsule).

**Phase 2 (1,000-10,000)**
- Introduce a standardized women's base formula (multi plus iron, folate and D, for example) made at a CMO: MOQ about 2,500-5,000 bottles or 100k-350k capsules. That is about $5k-25k per run plus about $2k-5k for stability and heavy-metal testing (estimate). Expect about 6-12 weeks lead time, an industry norm not sourced here.
- Personalize with 2-5 add-on SKUs through the pack vendor, or through a contract packer that will accept Vilo-owned inventory.
- Negotiate volume tiers ("packaging fees decrease as your volume grows").

**Phase 3 (10,000+)**
- **Option A, own cGMP pouching room** with 2-4 Parata/Yuyama/Tosho-class packagers. Used units cost $2.5k-50k; new units are quote-only, likely five to six figures each (unverified). Other costs:
  - canisters calibrated per SKU
  - vision inspection
  - FDA facility registration
  - a full-time quality unit
  - NSF/ANSI 455-2 (4-18 months, five-figure fees)
  - a 20-40 SKU formulary at about $135k-225k of inventory
- **Option B:** contract the same process to a dedicated packer, which avoids facility and staff fixed costs.
- **Target landed COGS:** about $17-35 per customer-month.
- **Indicative total startup capital for Phase 3:** about $400k-1M. This is an estimate: equipment, facility fit-out, 2-4 quality and operations hires for the first year, inventory, and certification.

**Across all phases**
- Per-customer lot traceability.
- Prop 65 lead summed per daily pack.
- A summer heat protocol: isolate probiotics and softgels, or choose shelf-stable formats.
- Returns are destroyed and logged.

### Gaps
- No vendor quotes were obtained (the search budget ran out and fetching vendor sites was blocked). Before committing, get quotes from OK Capsule (pack calculator), Persona white-label, two or three CMOs (e.g., Vitaquest, Lief Labs, NutraScience Labs, SMP Nutra, Makers Nutrition) and Parata/BD and Yuyama.
- Staffing costs, facility lease costs and insurance premiums were not researched.
